Why Ruby Kiwi?

Discover the three special qualities of Ruby Kiwi.

The red core of the flesh is proof that it is rich in anthocyanin, a powerful antioxidant.

Natural Vitamin C Bomb

Just one kiwi a day meets the recommended daily intake of Vitamin C for adults, making it a highly nutritious fruit.
